## How it works

The Charter is the class's own set of rules for AI use. [Every version is posted on the Charter page](/charter/), with the changes marked.

The class writes it inside limits Professor Wilk reserves. The closed list stays his: Tuesday seminars, the Sealed Position, the conference, and both Position Paper sittings. Inside the open zone the class decides what counts as disclosure, what a teammate can expect, and what happens when someone breaks the rules.

It is written in three sittings:

1. **The first draft.** Groups draft it in the second session.
2. **The amendment.** After the Exhibition, each team proposes one change that the build taught them, and the class votes.
3. **The Convention.** Teams bring proposed final clauses. The class debates and amends clause by clause, votes on each clause, and signs. The ratified text goes to the LEAD-AI Council.

In cycle 9 the reserved list itself is open to question. Who wrote it, who can change it, and whether you can contest it are the questions that cycle asks of every rulebook.

## What passing looks like

There is nothing to pass. The Charter is a class document, and it is not one of the contract's requirements.
