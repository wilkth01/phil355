## How it works

Right after the reading ticket, the class splits into groups of three. Each group gets a small case that no one has seen before.

One of you says what should happen. One presses with the five prompts. One watches. Then you rotate, so everyone takes each seat. The whole thing takes about twelve minutes.

At the first seminar, Professor Wilk takes the answering seat in front of the room before you try it.

This is the one place in the course where you think on your feet about something new.

## What passing looks like

Four-minute cases are never graded. They are practice for the conference, where the same five prompts are put to your Position Paper case.
