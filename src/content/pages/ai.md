## The rule in one line

Thursdays on, Tuesdays off.

Thursday labs are done with AI. Tuesday seminars are done without it, with laptops closed. Every session and every assignment on this site carries a marker that says which rule applies.

## Open, closed, required

**Open.** Use whatever you like, check it, and say so. Reading support, brainstorming, generating objections to your own view, checking whether a paragraph is clear, working out what you think. Any fact or citation that came out of a generative tool has to be verified against the source before it shows up in your work. Treat an unverified citation as a fabricated one.

**Closed.** Done without AI. The list is short on purpose: Tuesday seminars, the Sealed Position, the conference, and both Position Paper sittings.

**Required.** Using AI is part of the task. The Exhibition is built with it, and you report what you did and what the tool did.

## Disclosure

Every assignment you hand in carries a one-line note saying what you used and what for. "None" is a fine answer.

You bear final responsibility for what you write. If an AI tool introduces falsehoods, plagiarizes, or in some other way damages the work you submit, you are accountable.
