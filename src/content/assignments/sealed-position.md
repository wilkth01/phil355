## How it works

On the first day you answer five questions by hand, in about fifteen minutes. Your answers go into an envelope, and the envelope is sealed.

In the last week of class you open it, reread what you wrote in January, and write on the back what has changed. Then you compare with a partner.

The Position Paper is, in part, your answer to the person who wrote those five answers.

## What passing looks like

There is nothing to pass. The Sealed Position is not marked, and it is not one of the contract's requirements.
