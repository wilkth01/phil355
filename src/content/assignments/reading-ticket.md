## How it works

Every Tuesday seminar opens with a ticket. You get one question on the week's core reading and five minutes to answer it by hand, in four or five sentences.

The question is on the core reading only. It is one you can answer from the reading and not from a summary of it.

You may use your own handwritten notes. That is the reason to take notes by hand over the weekend. Laptops stay closed.

The question is never posted on this site.

## What passing looks like

A ticket passes or it does not. The contract counts how many you pass.
