After AI Week, you turn your exhibit's argument into 600 words for a campus reader. You revise it once, after Professor Wilk's comments. It is the only revised, public prose in the course.

Where it is published is to be announced.

The A tier requires both this and [your own case](/assignments/own-case/). The B tier requires one of the two.

The piece is handed in by the deadline and revised once after comments.
