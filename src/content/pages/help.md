## Title IX

Title IX of the Educational Amendments of 1972 prohibits discrimination based on sex in any educational institution that receives federal funding. Widener University does not tolerate sex discrimination of any kind including: sexual harassment; sexual assault, dating/domestic violence; stalking; and sexual exploitation. These incidents may interfere with or limit an individual's ability to benefit from or participate in the University's educational programs or activities. Please be aware that I am required to report any disclosures about such misconduct to the Title IX Coordinator.

If you (or someone you know) has experienced any form of sexual misconduct, please know that help and support are available. I strongly encourage all members of the community to take action, seek support, and report incidents of sexual misconduct to the Title IX Coordinator by using the reporting form located at [widener.edu/titleix](https://widener.edu/titleix), emailing <titleix@widener.edu>, or by calling 610-499-4553.
