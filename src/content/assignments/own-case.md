## How it works

Instead of choosing from the case bank, you write the case you will work on for the [Position Paper](/assignments/position-paper/): one page, set in your own field, with the facts a reader needs and the question it raises.

Finding a real case that is hard in the right way takes more understanding than picking one. The good ones join the case bank next year.

The A tier requires both this and the [public piece](/assignments/public-piece/). The B tier requires one of the two.

## What passing looks like

Your case is approved by the deadline. A proposal that is not ready is returned to you.
