## How it works

A Log is about 250 words in three parts:

1. Something that happened between you and an AI system this week.
2. One idea from the course, applied to it.
3. What the idea doesn't capture about what happened.

The third part is where the work is. A chatbot can write the first two parts. It can't write the third, because it wasn't there.

The Log is not a reading check. It is where you notice what these systems are doing in your own week. It is due on Wednesday night, after the seminar it reflects on.

Logs 7 and 8 fall in the Exhibition build, and they are about the build: what you did, what the AI did, what your team couldn't have done without it, and what it got wrong.

## What passing looks like

Each Log is marked successfully completed or not. The contract counts how many you complete.
